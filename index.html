1984

1984 by George Orwell is a classic novel that I have not finished yet, but it's incredibly mind-boggling. I got onto this book from a recommendation from my partner, and I am savouring it. The novel has incredible concepts and ideas that really warp and change one's idea of trust; the value of trust in this novel is the embodiment of giving your entire being, mind, and philosophy which should be what its about. However, 1984 challenges that simple definition of trust and displays it in a scenario where trust is survival to depict the true weight and value trust holds.
